One business, two parts that work together

Freelancing gives you an income now. Part of that income funds a network marketing business, which is the part designed to grow into larger earnings over the long run as your team grows.

First: earn

Freelancing

Sell a skill such as writing, design or tech support to clients around the world, on your own device or at our office.

  • Learn a skill clients are already paying for
  • Set up a Fiverr profile and gigs that get noticed
  • Win and deliver your first orders properly
  • Receive payments and withdraw them safely
Your earnings fund the next part
Then: build for the long run

Network marketing

Use part of your freelancing income to start a network marketing business. It takes longer to build, but as your team grows it can become a bigger source of income than freelancing alone.

  • Understand how the business actually works
  • Share products honestly, online and offline
  • Build and support a team of your own
  • Learn from leaders who are already doing it

Why freelancing comes first. Network marketing is where the bigger, long-term earnings can come from, but it takes money and time to build. When your freelancing pays for it, you don't have to borrow or use your savings, and you still have an income while the business grows.

Do I need any experience?

No. Many members start as complete beginners. We teach you a skill that pays and guide you step by step on how to earn from it.

Do I need my own phone or laptop?

Just the willingness to learn and stay consistent. If you don't have a phone or laptop, you can work on the devices available at our office.

How soon will I start earning?

That depends on your skill, your effort and the time you put in. We don't promise specific earnings. We do promise clear guidance and support while you do the work.

Why start with freelancing?

Growing a network marketing business has costs. Freelancing gives you your own income to cover them, so you don't need to borrow or spend your savings, and you keep earning while the business grows.

Do I have to do both?

The 2-in-1 model is how Assurgent Team works, and it's what we coach. Message us and we'll talk through how it fits your situation.

How do I join?

Send us a message on WhatsApp. Yuzzy or a team member will reply and guide you from there.
